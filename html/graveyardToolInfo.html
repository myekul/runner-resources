The DLC Graveyard puzzle expects the player to talk to the three climber NPCs on the podium, and read the hints in their dialogue to determine which graves to interact with. The grave order is randomized upon creation of a save file.

In a speedrunning context, the graveyard puzzle can be problematic because it requires you to read some dialogue and mentally process the solution, which can be time-consuming. To mitigate this, it's possible to look at just 1 word from each NPC to programmatically decipher the grave order.

During a run, talk to the podium NPCs left-to-right. For each one, look at their last dialogue box.
-For the leftmost NPC, look at the top right word.
-For the middle NPC, look at the bottom right word.
-For the rightmost NPC, look at the top right word again.

These words are unique across each possible message, meaning they directly correspond to each grave location. All you have to do is memorize those 3 words and select them on this tool to reveal the puzzle solution. Good luck!
